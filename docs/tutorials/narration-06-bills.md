## Notes on what's shown
- A pulsing orange ring circles whatever button or area is being talked about
  (checkbox, tiles, tabs, Pay/Work it out buttons), and a small dot tracks the
  real cursor throughout — added so viewers can see exactly what's being
  clicked or described instead of just watching the UI change.
- Logged in as `staff25`, a plain staff account.
- Test Student 10 is shown with bills already turned on and a week of bills issued, so
  there's real data to walk through — normally a freshly-enrolled student sees "No bills
  yet" until the following Monday.
- The Pay and Work-it-out modals are opened to show what they look like, then closed
  without submitting, so the demo data stays put for re-recording.
- All data is synthetic test data. No real students.
