## Notes on what's shown
- A pulsing orange ring circles whatever control is being talked about, and a small dot
  tracks the real cursor throughout.
- Logged in as `staff25`, a plain staff account.
- Hiding is additive, not exclusive — an item can carry several rules at once (say, hidden
  from yellow cards AND from one specific student), and it's only visible to a student when
  none of the item's rules match them.
- The demo "Puzzle Book" item and its hide rule are left in place after recording — safe to
  leave for a re-recording, or removed afterward as an admin (Edit/Delete are admin-only).
- All data is synthetic test data. No real students.
